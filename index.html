About Our Church

St. Francis Xavier's Church in Melakovilpatti is a vibrant Catholic community rooted in faith, hope, and love. For over 8 years, our parish has been a spiritual home for over 230 families, united in worship and service.

We believe in building a strong community that serves God and each other through prayer, fellowship and outreach. Everyone is warmly welcome to be part of our parish family.
