About Our Church

St. Francis Xavier's Church in Melakovilpatti is a vibrant Catholic community rooted in faith, hope, and love. Established as the 75th Parish of the Madurai Archdiocese on August 4, 2018, our parish is a spiritual home for over 230 families, united in worship and service.

Located in the serene green hills of Melakovilpatti near Batlagundu, our church is dedicated to St. Francis Xavier — the patron saint of India's missions, protector of soldiers, and healer of diseases. We welcome everyone warmly to our parish family.

Our Roots

Church History

🏔️

The Origin — ~250 Years Ago

About 250 years ago, the region then known as Nerthanjiyam (in present-day Thanjavur district) was devastated by severe triple floods. The floodwaters submerged the land as far as the eye could see, disrupting people's way of life completely. Forced to leave behind all their land and possessions, the people set out in search of a safe and suitable place to live.

🌿

Melakovilpatti — God's Promised Land

These migrants settled in a beautiful green hilly area surrounded by the rivers Manjalar, Marudanathi and Vaigai — a place they compared to God's promised land of Canaan. This place, today called Melakovilpatti (Melainagar), is near Batlagundu in Dindigul District. Early settlers engaged in farming, cattle rearing, and labour work. Later, they served in the Zamindar's palace, earning the village headman (Nattamai) title through the Zamindar's trust.

✝️

Arrival of Christianity

A faithful Christian named Savarimeuppan came and settled in Melakovilpatti. Under his influence and the guidance of dedicated parish priests, the Hindu people of the village gradually converted to the Christian faith. These converts were well cared for and nurtured spiritually by the parish fathers. The community then chose St. Francis Xavier as their patron saint, forming a strong and growing Christian community.

⛪

The Big Church — Built in 1948

Through the tireless efforts of Vattalagundu Parish Priest P.S. Anthoni and the wholehearted cooperation of the people, the main St. Francis Xavier Church was constructed in 1948. The entire community contributed with charcoal, chili, river sand, limestone and other materials, uniting together to build their beloved church.

🕯️

The Small Chapel — 1974

In 1974, the old Xavier Chapel was destroyed by fire. In its place, a small cross was erected to mark the sacred spot, keeping the memory and faith of the community alive.

🎉

New Parish Established — August 4, 2018

On August 4, 2018, Melakovilpatti was officially separated from the Vattalagundu Parish and established as the 75th new Parish of the Madurai Archdiocese. This was a momentous occasion for the entire community, marking a new chapter in the spiritual journey of St. Francis Xavier's Church.
